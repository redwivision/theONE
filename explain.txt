🍕 THE MOVIE PIZZA PARTY (Project Summary) 🍕

Imagine you are building the ultimate Movie Pizza Shop called "The One." Here is how it works:

1. THE KITCHEN (The Python Backend) 👨‍🍳
This is where all the work happens. We have a Chef (FastAPI) who waits for orders. 
- The Menu: We have lists of different "Vibes" (like Toppings!).
- The Recipe Book (OMDB API): If the Chef doesn't know a movie, he calls the Big Movie Recipe Book to get the details.
- The Trash Can (SQLite Database): If you say "I hate Pineapple pizza" (or a boring movie), the Chef remembers it and never makes it for you again.

2. THE DELIVERY APP (The Flutter Frontend) 📱
This is the screen you hold in your hand! 
- Right now, we just finished the "Phone Line."
- We made a Pizza Box (The Movie Model). If the kitchen sends us a messy pile of cheese and crust, our box organizes it into "Title," "Year," and "Poster" so it looks nice.

3. THE CONNECTION (The API Service) 📡
This is the phone call between your App and the Kitchen. 
- We just proved the phone line works! When you press a button, the kitchen tells the app which pizza is coming, and the app prints the name on the screen.

- App Face: WE ARE BUILDING THE BOXES NOW! 📦
